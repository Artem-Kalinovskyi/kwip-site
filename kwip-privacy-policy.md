# Kwip — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [TODO: publication date]

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Kwip** ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it on Android. Kwip is developed by Artem Kalinovskyi, an independent developer based in Ukraine, who is responsible for the game and for this policy.

In short: **the game does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.** Your progress is stored on your device. Some optional features rely on Google services, which process data under Google's own privacy policy.

## 1. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following **only on your device**:

- your current level, the board of the level in progress and your undo history;
- your star balance, lives (energy), hint stock and planet restoration progress;
- whether Kwip Premium is active on this device;
- the date of your last daily bonus;
- your settings (sound, music, language).

This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 2. Android Auto Backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may back up the game's saved data to your Google account, so your progress can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is performed and stored by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not have access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## 5. Purchases (Kwip Premium)

Kwip Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Google Play**. Your payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never receive or store them. We only receive a confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can unlock Kwip Premium and restore it if you reinstall. See the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## 8. Your rights

Because the game keeps your data on your device, you are always in control of it: you can delete it by clearing the game's data or uninstalling it. For data processed by Google or the ad provider, you can use the controls and rights described in their privacy policies. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data-protection laws, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing; contact us and we will help with any data we control.
