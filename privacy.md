# Kwip — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Kwip** ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it on Android. Kwip is developed by Artem Kalinovskyi, an independent developer based in Ukraine, who is responsible for the game and for this policy.

In short: **the game does not collect any personal information and does not send any data to us or to third parties.** Your progress is stored only on your device.

## 1. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following **only on your device**:

- your current level, the board of the level in progress and your undo history;
- your star balance, lives (energy), hint stock and planet restoration progress;
- the date of your last daily bonus;
- your settings (sound, music, language).

This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 2. Android Auto Backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may back up the game's saved data to your Google account, so your progress can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is performed and stored by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not have access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## 3. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
- The game does not show ads and does not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- The game does not offer in-app purchases.
- We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any.

## 4. Children

Kwip is a general-audience game with content suitable for all ages. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

## 5. Your rights

All of the game's data stays on your device, so you are always in control of it: you can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data or uninstalling the game. For data backed up by Android, you can use the controls described in Google's privacy policy. If you have any questions about your data, contact us.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information — for example, if optional ads, in-app purchases or cloud saves are added — this policy will be updated before those features become available, and the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
